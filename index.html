🥤 Lesson 21: Digital 7 Manipulative

Shake it • Spill it • Move it • Make a counting path

Seven counters! Move them or choose Counting Path.
Teacher routine: 🥤 Shake & Spill → ask “How many?” → Counting Path → click 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 → rearrange → “Is it still 7?”
In Counting Path mode, click the counters in order. In Move Counters mode, drag them anywhere.
